About NEUROSCRIPT

Bridging human handwriting with machine intelligence

NEUROSCRIPT is an enterprise handwritten character recognition platform designed to convert unconstrained handwritten documents into accurate digital text across English, Hindi, and Tamil. By combining state-of-the-art vision transformers, CRNN sequence models, and post-processing NLP tools, NEUROSCRIPT delivers seamless digital text extraction.
